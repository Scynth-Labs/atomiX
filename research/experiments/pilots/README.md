# Experiment-alpha pilot evidence

Accepted AX-04 pilot reports live here as
`participant-<pseudonym>-<yyyy-mm-dd>.md`. Start from
[`docs/experiment-alpha-pilot-template.md`](../../../docs/experiment-alpha-pilot-template.md)
and retain its prompts so omissions remain visible during review.

Two reports are required, each from someone who did not implement the
experiment runner or walkthrough. Across the two reports, at least one must
exercise Path A (native only) and at least one must exercise Path B (native and
RTL). Each report must include the exact source revision, separate setup/build/
interaction timing, time to the first understood comparison, every failure and
source of help, a changed declared choice, another record's replay outcome, and
the participant's own tradeoff and evidence-limit explanations.

Keep reports compact. Do not commit generated build trees, executables, bundles,
or full logs; quote only the output needed to explain a failure or conclusion.
Do not include email addresses or other personal details that the participant
does not want in the repository.
